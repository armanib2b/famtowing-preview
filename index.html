Extras, only when the job needs them

No surprise fees. Here's every one.

Some things we can't know until we see the car. If one of these comes up, dispatch tells you before we do it.

  • Keys locked inside

    If the car needs a tow and the keys are locked in it, we have to open it first with our lockout tools.

    $75

  • No key: wheels turned or stuck in Park

    Without a key we can't straighten the steering wheel or shift into neutral, so the car won't roll straight onto the bed. That takes go-jacks or skates.

    Quoted first

  • Wheel broken, missing or locked

    A wheel that's hanging, gone or won't turn needs dollies, skates or go-jacks under that corner. Dispatch tells you the charge before we use them.

    Quoted first

  • Stuck off the road

    Ditch, mud or an embankment means winching it out before the tow. Light duty rate, billed by the hour.

    $65/hour

  • Storage at our yard

    Only if we hold the vehicle for you. Light duty rate.

    $30/day

  • Canceled after we're on the way

    If the truck is already rolling and you cancel, or nobody's there when we arrive.

    $35

Common misconceptions

Towing myths, busted

Stuff people believe about tow trucks that just isn't true. At least not with us.

MythYou need AAA or insurance to get a tow.

TruthNope. Call us, get one price, pay us directly. No membership, no claim number.

MythTow companies won't tell you the price until they show up.

TruthOurs is on your screen before we roll, line by line. Tap Get a Price and see.

MythA dead battery or flat tire means a tow.

TruthUsually not. A jump, your spare or a little gas fixes it right there, for $75.

MythTowing will damage my car.

TruthNot on a flatbed done right. Your car rides on the deck with its wheels off the road.

MythYou'll wait hours for a tow truck.

TruthWe're local in Lithonia. Our reviewers often mention help showing up in about half an hour.

MythElectric cars tow like any other car.

TruthEVs need extra care for the battery and drivetrain. That's why we price them on a quick call.

Questions

Straight answers

Are there any hidden fees?

No. You see your price before we roll. Extras only apply when the job needs them, like a locked car, a broken wheel or winching out of a ditch, and they're all listed on our prices page.

Do I need a membership or insurance to use you?

No. Most of our customers pay us directly. Call, get one straight price, and we come out. No membership, motor club or claim number needed.

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes. Towing and roadside assistance run 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across Lithonia, Stonecrest, Stone Mountain, Decatur, Tucker, Conyers and Loganville. Whenever you call, someone answers.

How fast can you get to me?

It depends on traffic and where you are, but we know these roads and can usually reach you faster than the big national providers. Dispatch gives you a real ETA when you call.
