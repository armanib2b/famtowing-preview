Pricing questions

Straight answers about money

Still not sure? Call (470) 600-2326. We'll tell you the price before we roll.

How is my towing price figured?

Three parts: the $100 hook-up, the drive to you (first 10 miles free, then $2.75 a mile from our Lithonia yard) and the miles to your drop-off (first 5 free, then $4 a mile). The Get a Price button does the math for you.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The only extras are for things the job actually needs, like opening a locked car, dollies for a broken wheel, winching a car out of a ditch, or storage. They're all listed on this page, and dispatch tells you before we do anything extra.

My keys are locked in the car and it needs a tow. Is that extra?

Yes. We have to open the car with our lockout tools before we can tow it, so a $75 lockout is added. If you have a spare key nearby, it's worth grabbing.

I don't have the key. Does that change the price?

It can. Without the key we can't unlock the steering or put the car in neutral, so if the wheels are turned or it's stuck in Park, we use go-jacks or skates to load it. Dispatch tells you the charge first. If the key is anywhere nearby, bring it and you'll likely skip the extra.

My wheel is broken or came off. What happens?

We put dollies, skates or go-jacks under that corner so the car can be loaded safely. That adds a charge, and dispatch tells you the amount before we use them.

My car is stuck in a ditch or mud. Can you get it out?

Yes. Winching is $65 an hour for light-duty vehicles, on top of the tow. Tell dispatch where it's stuck and how far off the road.

Do you charge for storage?

Only if we hold your vehicle at our yard. It's $30 a day for light-duty vehicles.

What if I cancel?

No charge if you cancel before we roll. If the truck is already on the way, or nobody is there when we arrive, it's $35.

Is my pickup or SUV light duty?

Yes, if it's a regular car, SUV, van or half-ton pickup (up to 10,000 lbs). Those use the prices on this page. F-350s, 3500s, duallys and box trucks are medium duty: $150 hook-up, and $3 a mile after the first 10 free to reach you. The Get a Price button handles both.

Why don't you list a price for Teslas and electric cars?

Every EV is different. The battery can catch fire if it's damaged, many can't simply be put in neutral, and they have to ride fully on the flatbed. The right setup depends on the model and the situation, so we give you an exact price on the phone instead of guessing online.

Do you tow motorcycles?

Not right now. We're adding the equipment for motorcycles soon. Call and we'll let you know.
